Our story

A boutique built around discovery.

Velvet Vine is a wine boutique on Cherry Lane in Ventura, California, created for people who enjoy discovering wine, whether they know exactly what they’re looking for or simply want to explore.

The boutique is about presentation and appreciation: wines arranged with care, room to browse, and the chance to ask about any bottle that catches your eye.

Our philosophy

Four quiet principles.

Client to confirmDesign themes describing the boutique’s outlook, not operational or service claims.

  1. I

    Thoughtful Selection

    Every bottle on the shelf is there for a reason, presented so its character is easy to understand.

  2. II

    Wine Discovery

    Exploring something new is half the pleasure. Familiar favourites sit alongside bottles waiting to be found.

  3. III

    The Boutique Experience

    A calm, intimate space where browsing feels unhurried and questions are always welcome.

  4. IV

    Appreciation of Craft

    Respect for the care that goes into every bottle, from the vineyard to the glass.
